| B.5.1 | DD | **Structure the flagship project** — Goal: a 30-minute-deep, NDA-safe narrative of one project (plus a backup). · Prompt: the learner fills the 8-part deep-dive outline (problem · role/scope · architecture · hardest decision · numbers · failures/ops · what I'd change · cross-team) in `behavioral/stories/deep-dive-<slug>.md`; Claude asks for every missing number and every "why". | B.5.2 | DD | **Deep-dive rehearsal as a design review** — Goal: defend the project like a system design. · Prompt: 25 min, spoken: 3-min overview + diagram, then Claude plays a senior interviewer probing: "why this datastore?", "what happens when X fails?", "how would you scale it 10×?", "what would you redo?", "what did *you* write?".
