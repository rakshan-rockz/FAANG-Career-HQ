# Behavioral Phase 4 — Other Company Frameworks

**Window:** Jan–Feb 2027 (months 4–5). **Est:** 5 h.
Same fact base, new framing. Every session: (1) learn the framework and round format, (2) map stories to
it (matrix column), (3) answer 3–4 classic questions spoken in that company's style, probed.

> Everything company-specific below comes from public material and candidate reports and **changes over
> time: verify** against official career pages, recruiter prep emails (they usually send a prep guide:
> read it), and your own debriefs. Record verified facts in `career/company-notes/<company>.md`.

## Framework reference

| Company | Round | Framework / signals (verify) | Classic questions | Style notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Google** | "Googleyness & Leadership" (G&L), usually one dedicated round; also general behavioral probes in HM/team-match calls | Googleyness: thriving in ambiguity, bias to action, collaboration, intellectual humility (admitting you're wrong), putting the user first, doing the right thing, conscientiousness. Leadership = *emergent* leadership (stepping up without a title), influencing, helping the team succeed | "Tell me about a time you had to work with incomplete information." · "…you changed your mind based on feedback." · "…a team member wasn't pulling their weight." · Hypotheticals: "What would you do if your team disagreed on a launch?" | Structured, often includes **hypothetical** "what would you do" questions (answer with principles + a past example). Hiring committee reads the write-up: specificity matters more than charisma |
| **Microsoft** | Behavioral questions inside most rounds; often an **"As Appropriate" (AA)** final round with a senior leader (verify per team) | Culture: growth mindset (learn-it-all vs know-it-all), customer obsessed, diversity & inclusion, One Microsoft (collaboration across boundaries), making a difference | "Tell me about a time you failed and what you learned." · "…you learned from someone very different from you." · "…you collaborated across teams." · "Why Microsoft?" | Warm, conversational; growth-mindset language and **CARL** Learning endings score well; team-specific (the hiring team drives the loop) |
| **Meta** | One dedicated **behavioral** interview (a.k.a. "Jedi"), ~45 min, at E4/E5 (verify) | Signals (commonly reported): resolving conflict, embracing ambiguity, driving results / impact, growing continuously (feedback, self-awareness), communicating effectively; values: move fast, focus on long-term impact, build awesome things, be direct and respect your colleagues | "Tell me about the project you're most proud of." · "…a conflict with a coworker." · "…the most constructive feedback you received." · "…a time you had to push through ambiguity." · "What would your manager say is your biggest area of growth?" | Fast-paced, many follow-ups, **level calibration** is explicit (scope of impact, how many people influenced): state scope clearly |
| **Atlassian** | A dedicated **values interview** (pass/fail, separate from technical rounds) + a management/HM round (verify) | Values: **Open company, no bullshit** · **Build with heart and balance** · **Don't #@!% the customer** · **Play, as a team** · **Be the change you seek** | "Tell me about a time you gave candid feedback." · "…you put the customer first at a cost." · "…you balanced speed and quality / work and life." · "…you helped a teammate succeed." · "…you changed something that bothered you." | Values interviewer is often from another team; one story per value; honesty and **balance** (not hero stories) score well; failing it fails the loop |
| **Uber** | Behavioral in the HM round and "collaboration"/culture round (verify) | Cultural values (a 2018 set; **verify the current wording**): build globally, live locally; customer obsessed; celebrate differences; do the right thing, period; act like owners; persevere; value ideas over hierarchy; make big bold bets | "Tell me about a time you persevered through a setback." · "…you challenged a senior person's idea." · "…you owned something end to end." | Ownership and perseverance; pragmatic; expects numbers |
| **Indian product cos** (Flipkart, PhonePe, Razorpay, Swiggy, Zomato, Walmart Global Tech, Adobe, Salesforce, Oracle, Intuit) | Usually a **hiring-manager round** (often also a system/project deep dive) and sometimes an HR/"culture fit" round | Varies (verify on each careers page): recurring themes are ownership, bias for action, customer-first, integrity, collaboration, learning agility | "Walk me through your current project." · "Why are you leaving?" · "What's your notice period / can you join early?" · "Tell me about a production issue you handled." · "Where do you see yourself in 3 years?" | The HM round decides **level** and often **band**; deep technical project questions dominate; notice period and CTC questions appear early (see B.6.3) |
