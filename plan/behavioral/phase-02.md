# Behavioral Phase 2 — Crafting the Story Bank

**Window:** Nov–Dec 2026 (months 2–3), ~1 session every 1–2 weeks alongside the heavy DSA load.
**Est:** 8.5 h. Each ST session runs `/story` on two stories: **mine → STAR/CARL draft → quantify →
probe (P1–P5) → tighten → speak it (timed)**. The learner writes/dictates every sentence; Claude critiques
structure, "I"-ratio, metrics and reflection, and asks the probes.

Row fields: **Goal** · **Prompt/exercise** · **Covers** · **Output** · **Done-when**.

| ID | Type | Session | Est h |
|---|---|---|---:|
| B.2.1 | ST | **Flagship project + one conflict story** — Goal: craft the two stories that appear in almost every loop. · Prompt: `/story` on the flagship project (behavioral version, not yet the full deep dive) and the strongest peer-conflict story. Headline first; Situation ≤ 2 sentences; Action as 3–4 "I" beats with reasoning; Result with numbers; Learning. · Covers: themes 1, 2; the difference between conflict about *people* and about *technical approach* (prefer the latter); showing empathy for the other side. · Output: `behavioral/stories/01-*.md`, `02-*.md` with STAR text, numbers + how measured, P1–P5 answers, 30 s/2 min versions. · Done-when: both stories spoken in 2–3 min with ≥ 1 number each and written P1–P5 answers; status 🟨 | 1.25 |
| B.2.2 | ST | **Manager disagreement (right & wrong) stories** — Goal: show backbone *and* humility. · Prompt: `/story` on "I disagreed with my manager and was right" and "…and was wrong / I disagreed and committed". Probe P6: "Why not escalate?", "Did it hurt the relationship?". · Covers: themes 3, 14; Amazon *Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit*; data-driven disagreement; committing fully after losing the argument; not badmouthing the manager. · Output: stories 03, 04. · Done-when: both 🟨; neither contains blame; each has a clear "what I did after the decision" | 1.25 |
| B.2.3 | ST | **Failure/mistake + incident stories** — Goal: turn the scariest questions into strengths. · Prompt: `/story` on the best failure story (CARL) and the security/quality/production incident. Incident told as detection → triage → mitigation → root cause → prevention → what changed in process. · Covers: themes 4, 10; right-sizing failure; "the fix to the system, not just the bug"; blameless framing; security incidents at NDA-safe detail. · Output: stories 05, 06. · Done-when: both 🟨; failure story's Learning includes a later situation where it was applied | 1.25 |
| B.2.4 | ST | **Ambiguity + deadline stories** — Goal: show judgement when information or time is short. · Prompt: `/story` on the ambiguity story (how you created clarity: questions asked, assumptions written down, prototypes, stakeholders aligned) and the deadline story (what you cut, what you refused to cut, how you communicated risk). · Covers: themes 5, 6, 13; Meta "embracing ambiguity", Google comfort with ambiguity, Amazon *Bias for Action* / *Deliver Results*; scope vs quality trade-offs made explicit. · Output: stories 07, 08. · Done-when: both 🟨 with the decision point and rejected options explicit | 1.25 |
| B.2.5 | ST | **Leadership-without-authority + mentoring stories** — Goal: show SDE-2 (and emerging senior) scope. · Prompt: `/story` on influencing another team / driving a cross-team change, and on mentoring/onboarding someone (what they could do after that they couldn't before). · Covers: themes 7, 8; Amazon *Earn Trust*, *Hire and Develop the Best*; Google emergent leadership; influence tactics (data, prototypes, 1:1s, writing a doc). · Output: stories 09, 10. · Done-when: both 🟨; the mentoring story has an observable outcome for the mentee | 1.25 |
| B.2.6 | ST | **Customer impact + raising the bar / learning fast** — Goal: complete the bank (and fill any gap from B.1.5). · Prompt: `/story` on the customer story (the customer's problem in their terms, what you did beyond the ticket, their outcome) and a process/tooling/quality improvement or learn-fast story; plus any theme still without a primary story. · Covers: themes 9, 11, 12; Amazon *Customer Obsession*, *Insist on the Highest Standards*, *Learn and Be Curious*, *Invent and Simplify*; Atlassian "Don't #@!% the customer". · Output: stories 11, 12 (+13/14 if gaps). · Done-when: coverage matrix has a 🟨 primary for all 14 themes | 1.25 |
| B.2.7 | R | **Story-bank review: cold retell at three lengths** — Goal: prove the bank is usable, not just written. · Prompt: Claude gives 8 unlabeled questions in random order; the learner picks a story and answers spoken, timed (2–3 min); then 3 of them at 30 s and 1 at 5 min. Quick rubric per answer. · Covers: story selection under pressure (which story fits which question), re-headlining, length control; top 2 weak rubric categories → `progress/weak-areas.md`. · Output: `behavioral/mocks/<date>-B.2.7-review.md`; story statuses updated; review-queue rows for every 🟨 story (+2/+7/+21/+60). · Done-when: 8/8 questions answered with an appropriate story; average ≥ 65/100 | 1 |
