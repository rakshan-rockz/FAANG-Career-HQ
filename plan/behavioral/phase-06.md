# Behavioral Phase 6 — Hard Questions

**Window:** Mar 2027 (month 6), just before applications open. **Est:** 3 h.
These are the questions that end loops early when answered badly. Every answer is **true**, short, and
rehearsed; none badmouths QNu Labs; none reveals a negotiation position by accident.

Row fields: **Goal** · **Prompt/exercise** · **Covers** · **Output** · **Done-when**.

| ID | Type | Session | Est h |
|---|---|---|---:|
| B.6.1 | HQ | **Tell me about yourself · Why are you leaving? · Why this company? · Why now?** — Goal: a crisp opening and a "pull, not push" motivation. · Prompt: the learner drafts and speaks: a 60–90 s "tell me about yourself" (present → past highlights → why this role), "why leaving" (what you want: scale, product breadth, engineering bar, learning; never "my company is bad" or "only money"), and a per-company "why us" template filled for the top 5 targets (product, engineering problems, something specific learned from research). · Covers: pull vs push framing; tailoring per company; honesty (the real reasons, framed constructively); consistency with the resume and LinkedIn. · Output: `behavioral/stories/hard-questions.md` §1. · Done-when: all spoken in time; no negative statement about the employer; "why us" is specific for top 5 | 1 |
| B.6.2 | HQ | **Weaknesses · biggest failure · conflict curveballs · gaps & sensitive points** — Goal: honest answers that still build confidence. · Prompt: a real weakness with the mitigation in progress and evidence; the biggest failure (from the bank, CARL); curveballs ("your manager would say…?", "a time you were rude/impatient", "a time you got negative feedback", "have you ever missed a deadline?"); any gap (career gap, short stint, no degree/low CGPA, domain switch) explained in one neutral sentence + what you did. · Covers: bounded weakness choice (real, not disqualifying for the role); self-awareness signals; not over-explaining. · Output: `hard-questions.md` §2. · Done-when: each answer ≤ 90 s, specific, with a mitigation or learning | 1 |
| B.6.3 | HQ | **Salary expectations · current CTC · notice period · counter-offers · relocation** — Goal: say nothing that weakens the negotiation, and nothing untrue. · Prompt: role-play a recruiter screen: "What's your current CTC?", "Expected CTC?", "Notice period? Can you join in 30 days?", "Any other offers?", "Would you accept a counter-offer?". The learner answers; Claude critiques phrasing. · Covers: India norms (current CTC is usually asked and verified by payslips/BGV: state it accurately, fixed vs variable split); deferring the expected number until the level/band is known, or giving a researched range anchored on the role, not on current pay; notice-period facts + buyout/early-release options (see `career/negotiation.md`); stating competing processes truthfully; counter-offer stance. · Output: `hard-questions.md` §3 + phrasing bank in `career/negotiation.md` §Scripts. · Done-when: every question answered truthfully without naming an expected number before the band is known (unless forced; then the researched range) | 1 |
