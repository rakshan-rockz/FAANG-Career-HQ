# Intake (H.0.1)

~45–60 min. Facts only, from the learner. Ask **a few questions at a time**; probe vague answers.

1. Explain in 3 lines: these facts set the level target, the resign-by date, the application window and
   how hours are split; nothing here reduces course content.
2. Career facts: exact title, total YOE and at QNu, previous roles; what they own today (scope, team size);
   level estimate (SDE-1 vs SDE-2 evidence); domain strengths (NDA-safe).
3. Compensation: current CTC 22 LPA structure (fixed / variable / other) — only what they want to record;
   define the **target** (30–35 LPA as fixed, year-1 total, or 4-yr average?).
4. **Notice period**: from the appointment letter/HR policy (not memory): days, buyout clause, early-release
   practice, leave-encashment offset. Compute: resign-by ≈ 2027-06-30 unless notice/joining goals say
   otherwise; state the joining window.
5. Targets: go through the candidate list (Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Uber, Atlassian, Meta, Flipkart,
   PhonePe, Razorpay, Swiggy, Zomato, Walmart Global Tech, Adobe, Salesforce, Oracle, Intuit, others);
   rank into **Dream / Target / Practice** with a reason each; location/relocation; remote preferences.
6. Availability per weekday/weekend; known crunches (releases, audits), travel, family, health; preferred
   study times. Check the 25–28 h/week assumption against reality and say so if it doesn't fit.
7. History: past interviews and outcomes; resume and LinkedIn state; any referral contacts already known.
8. Behavioral self-assessment (1–5 table) and one-line "why switch" in their words.
9. Write `progress/profile.md`; update `career/company-notes/README.md` tiers and `career/applications.md`
   batch table (tiers only); resolve or date the open items in `DECISIONS.md` §Open; set STATUS
   (Started date, resign-by, applications-from, Next = B.1.1). Tell the learner what the mentor needs to
   finalise `plan/SWITCH-PLAN.md` (it stays the mentor's document).
