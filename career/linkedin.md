# LinkedIn (CO.2.1)

Goal: recruiters searching "C++ SDE-2 Bangalore distributed systems security" find you and reply.
Everything written by the learner; consistent with the resume (dates, titles) because BGV and recruiters compare.

## Checklist
- [ ] **Photo** (clear, professional) and a simple banner
- [ ] **Headline** (≤ 220 chars): role + strengths + domain, e.g. shape: "Software Engineer (C++) · Systems & security · Post-quantum crypto · Distributed systems" (learner's words)
- [ ] **About** (3 short paragraphs): what you build, 2–3 proof points with numbers, what you're looking for (optional)
- [ ] **Experience**: 3–5 bullets per role from resume v2; product context in one line
- [ ] **Skills**: top 3 pinned (C++, Distributed Systems, Cryptography/Security or as fits); 15–30 total, all real
- [ ] **Featured**: a project, a post, a talk (optional)
- [ ] **Open to work**: *recruiters only* (not the public green frame) · roles, locations, start date
- [ ] **Location** set to target city; **custom URL** (`linkedin.com/in/firstname-lastname`)
- [ ] Education, certifications; recommendations (1–2 from seniors, optional)
- [ ] Settings: turn off "share profile updates" before editing heavily (so the employer isn't notified)
- [ ] Activity: ~1 thoughtful post/month or comments in your domain (optional, not a priority)
