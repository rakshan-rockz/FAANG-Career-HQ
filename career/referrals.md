# Referrals (CO.3.2)

A referral typically converts to a recruiter screen far more often than a cold application (verify with
your own funnel). Ask early (2–6 weeks before you want the call), make it easy, follow up, say thanks.

## Who to ask (best first)
1. Engineers you've worked with who are now at the company (they can vouch)
2. College alumni at the company (esp. on the target team/org)
3. 2nd-degree LinkedIn connections via a mutual friend's intro
4. Community contacts (meetups, open-source, conferences, Discords)
5. Cold employees on the team (lower yield; personalise heavily)
6. Recruiters (for the process, not the referral)

## What to send (make it a 2-minute task for them)
Job ID/link · resume PDF · 3-line pitch (who you are, why this role, 1 proof point) · permission to decline.

## Templates (adapt into the learner's voice; never mass-send identical text)
**Ex-colleague / friend**
> Hi <name>, hope <team/life> is going well! I'm starting to look for SDE-2 roles and <company>'s <team/role>
> (<job ID/link>) looks like a strong fit: I've been <1-line proof: e.g. building high-throughput C++ crypto
> services at QNu>. Would you be comfortable referring me? Resume attached; totally fine if not.

**Alumnus / 2nd-degree**
> Hi <name>, I'm <you>, <college/mutual> alum, currently a C++ engineer at QNu Labs working on <area>. I'm
> interested in <role, job ID> at <company>. Could I ask you 2–3 quick questions about the team, and if it
> seems a fit, would you consider referring me? Resume attached. Thanks for reading!

**Follow-up (after ~5–7 days, once)**
> Hi <name>, just bumping this in case it got buried. No worries at all if it's not possible.

**Thank-you / close the loop**
> Thanks again for the referral. Update: <got a screen / loop scheduled / result>. Really appreciate it.

## Cadence
Day 0 ask → day 5–7 one follow-up → then stop. After a referral: thank on the day; update at each stage.
Offer help back (review their resume, refer them later).
